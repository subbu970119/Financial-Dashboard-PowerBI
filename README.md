# Financial Dashboard (Power BI)

A 3-page Power BI report covering company sales, balance sheet and cash flow.

## Pages
1. **Executive Dashboard** – Total Revenue, Total Expenses, Net Profit, Profit Margin %, revenue vs expenses by month, expense breakdown, revenue by customer.
2. **Balance Sheet Dashboard** – Assets, Liabilities, Debt Ratio, Net Worth, assets by category, liabilities distribution, net worth by month.
3. **Cash Flow Dashboard** – Cash In, Cash Out, Net Cash Flow, monthly trend and cash flow distribution.

## Data Model
Tables: `Sales`, `Expenses`, `Assets`, `Liabilities`, `CashFlow`, `Calendar` (date table).

## Tools
Power BI Desktop, DAX measures, Power Query, data modelling.

## Key Insights
3-4 real findings, for example “Highest profit month was X” or “Expenses are mostly Y”.
